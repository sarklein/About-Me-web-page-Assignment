Favorite Restaurant

My favorite restaurant will forever and always be a deli sandwhich shop. I have so many, but
I will list my favorite one currently. This particular sandwhich shop is my favorite because
of the variety of sandwhiches to choose from. The recipe combos are all delicious and the
bread is baked to perfection. Ingredients are fresh and proportion size is just right!

The book gives a detail description on the types of marine life you will find within the central coast tide pools.
